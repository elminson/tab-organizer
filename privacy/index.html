AI Tab Organizer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

AI Tab Organizer ("the extension") is a browser extension that groups your open Chrome tabs into tab groups using an AI model you choose. This policy explains exactly what data the extension handles and where it goes. The developer operates no server and never receives, stores, or sells your data.

What the extension accesses

  • Open tab titles and URLs — read only when you click "Organize my tabs," and only to categorize those tabs into groups.
  • Your AI provider API key — the key you enter in Settings for your chosen provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic).
  • Your preferences — selected provider, optional model name, and toggle settings (collapse groups, include pinned tabs).

How the data is used

  • When you click "Organize my tabs," the extension sends the titles and URLs of your open tabs, together with your API key, directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected, over HTTPS. The provider returns a suggested grouping, which the extension applies as Chrome tab groups.
  • Your API key and preferences are stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. They are never transmitted anywhere except, in the case of the API key, to the provider you chose in order to authenticate your own request.
  • Tab titles and URLs are processed in memory to build the request and are not stored or logged by the extension.

Third-party AI providers

The extension only contacts the single provider you select. Data you send is handled under that provider's own privacy policy:

  • Google Gemini — ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • OpenAI — openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic — anthropic.com/legal/privacy

If you choose the "None" provider, no data leaves your browser at all — grouping is done locally by website domain.

What the extension does NOT do

  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry.
  • No developer-operated server; the developer never receives your data.
  • No selling or transferring of user data to third parties.
  • No use of your data for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending.
  • No collection of personal, financial, health, or location information.

Data retention and control

Your settings and API key remain on your device until you change or remove them. You can clear them anytime by removing values in Settings or uninstalling the extension, which deletes all locally stored data.

Children

The extension is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Material changes will be reflected in the extension's store listing.
